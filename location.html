Location

Trinity Hospital in Kampala is situated within the dynamic urban fabric of Uganda's capital, a city known for its concentration of major medical facilities such as Mulago National Referral Hospital and Kampala Hospital Its Kampala location places it close to key transport corridors, allowing patients to reach the facility easily amid the city's busy commercial and residential zones.
